General Instructions

This practice test copies the look of the NTA computer-based test (CBT) screen so that you can practise in the same way. It is not an official NTA test. Verify the current exam pattern and marking with the official NTA notice.

  1. Total questions: · Each question carries 2 marks · Duration: minutes · No negative marking (as in the current UGC NET; verify with the NTA notice).
  2. The clock at the top right shows the time left. The test is submitted automatically when the time is over.
  3. The Question Palette on the right shows the status of each question:
    • 1 You have not visited the question yet.
    • 2 You have not answered the question.
    • 3 You have answered the question.
    • 4 You have NOT answered the question, but have marked it for review.
    • 5 You have answered the question and marked it for review. Such answers WILL be considered for evaluation.
  4. To answer: click an option, then click SAVE & NEXT. If you move to another question without saving, your answer is not saved.
  5. SAVE & MARK FOR REVIEW saves your answer and marks the question. MARK FOR REVIEW & NEXT marks it without an answer. CLEAR removes your answer.
  6. Click a number in the palette to go straight to that question.
  7. Your progress is saved in this browser. If the page closes, open it again and click “Resume”.
